# Project Summary

## Title
Ice Hockey Match Analytics & Winner Prediction

## Objective
Analyze historical ice hockey results and evaluate whether pre-match team history and team identities help classify home-team wins.

## Dataset
9,282 match records and 30 columns, with match dates, teams, final scores, period-level scores, overtime fields, and winner.

## Workflow
1. Data validation and missing-value review
2. EDA and visual storytelling
3. Leakage-aware historical team-form feature engineering
4. Chronological holdout split
5. Logistic Regression and Random Forest
6. Classification metrics and confusion matrix
7. Exportable prediction and comparison tables

## Important
